RESTful API Endpoints

Zero external dependencies. Sub-50ms execution on Vercel Serverless Edge infrastructure.

GET /v1/almanac/daily

Complete daily almanac package: moon phase, illumination, tropical zodiac sign, fertility status, USDA zone, frost dates, and folklore weather proverb.

Params: ?zip=45402&date=2026-05-15
GET /v1/almanac/best-days

Monthly calendar of optimal dates for agricultural and homestead chores (planting, fishing, setting eggs, pruning, brewing, grafting).

Params: ?month=5&year=2026
GET /v1/almanac/crops

Comprehensive planting schedule tailored to a specific USDA zone or ZIP code, incorporating moon alignment rules and companion planting pairs.

Params: ?zip=45402 or ?zone=6a
GET /v1/almanac/lunar

High-precision astronomical ephemeris: moon phase name, lunar age, percent illumination, tropical zodiac sign, element, and fertility rating.

Params: ?date=2026-05-15
GET /v1/almanac/frost

Resolves any 5-digit US ZIP code to its USDA Plant Hardiness Zone (3a through 11a), spring median frost date, fall first frost date, and growing season days.

Params: ?zip=45402
GET /v1/almanac/solunar

Solunar wildlife tables: calculates major (transit/underfoot) and minor (rise/set) feeding periods, overall day score, and star rating for hunters and anglers.

Params: ?zip=45402&date=2026-05-15
